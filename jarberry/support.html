How to play

Drag to aim, lift to drop. Two identical fruits merge into the next fruit. If fruit stays above the dashed line for three seconds, the jar overflows and the run ends. Merges fill the jam meter; when it is full, tap it for a Jam Burst that clears the smallest fruit.

Jars, themes and skins

New jars, themes and fruit skins unlock as you play: finish runs, discover fruit and set high scores. Pick a jar for your next run in Settings or on the game-over card.

Restore a purchase

If you bought “Remove Ads + Supporter” and it is missing, open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.

Ads and privacy

Rewarded ads are always optional. You can change ad consent in Settings → Ad Privacy Choices where required. Read the privacy policy.

Contact

Found a bug or have an idea? Email claudiurasteanu@icloud.com and include your device model and iOS version. We usually reply within a few days.
