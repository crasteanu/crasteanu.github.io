Privacy Policy

Vialflow is a potion-sorting puzzle game. Your progress stays on your device. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some data as described below.

Data we handle

Vialflow has no accounts and no server of its own. Your unlocked levels, best scores and times, Daily Brew streak, Codex discoveries, themes, settings and the level in progress are stored only on your device. The game also keeps simple play counters (for example, how many levels you cleared and how many hints you used today) on your device; they are never sent to us.

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

Advertising

Vialflow is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos you choose to watch for an extra hint, and occasional full-screen ads between levels. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device’s advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostic data.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Vialflow asks for your consent through Google’s consent form before ads are personalised. iOS also asks whether Vialflow may track you; if you decline, ads are not personalised using your advertising identifier. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases

The optional “Remove Ads” purchase is processed by Apple. We never see your payment details; the app receives only Apple’s confirmation that the purchase is owned, so it can turn off ads between levels and make hints unlimited.

Your choices

  • Change ad consent at any time in Vialflow: Levels → Sanctum → Ad privacy choices (shown where required).
  • Change tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Reset your advertising identifier or limit ad tracking in iOS settings.
  • Delete all game data by deleting the app.

Retention

Game data stays on your device until you delete the app. Data collected by Google for advertising is retained under Google’s own policies.

Children and changes

Vialflow is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
