How to play

Tap a vial to pick it up, then tap another to pour. You can pour onto the same colour or into an empty vial, and a finished vial seals itself. Clear a level at or under par to earn three stars. If no pour can make progress, the game tells you so — use Undo or Restart.

Hints

You get three free hints each day. When they run out you can watch a short ad for one more, or buy Remove Ads for unlimited hints.

Levels, bosses and the Daily Brew

The campaign has 100 levels across four chapters, with a boss brew at the end of each and concealed brews from chapter two. Open Levels to choose a level or play today’s Daily Brew. Themes and potion styles unlock as you earn perfect clears.

Restore a purchase

If you bought “Remove Ads” and it is missing, open Levels → Sanctum in the game and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.

Ads and privacy

Rewarded ads are always optional. You can change ad consent in Levels → Sanctum → Ad privacy choices where required. Read the privacy policy.
